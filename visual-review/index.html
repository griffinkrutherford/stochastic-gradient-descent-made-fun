Scores are the coding agent’s subjective visual assessment, not independent student or teacher ratings. Numerical correctness, accessibility behaviors, and interaction checks are tested separately. Open an image to inspect the original full-resolution capture.

Rubric and changes made during review

Aesthetics: framing/composition (30), lighting/color/depth (25), visual hierarchy (25), controls/layout (20). Interpretability: accurate mathematical mapping (35), axes/labels (25), numerical explanation (25), analogy limits (15).

The review prompted fixes for reverse-view occlusion, camera clipping, crowded labels, phone canvas resizing, overlapping insets, and original-bowl phone framing. Desktop captures use Chrome at 1440 × 1100; phone captures use 390 × 844. Decorative motion is frozen for repeatable inspection, except the bowl is briefly run and then paused to show settled marbles.
